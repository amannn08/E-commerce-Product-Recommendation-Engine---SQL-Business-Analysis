# E-commerce Product Recommendation Engine - SQL Business Analysis

## Project Overview
**Objective**: Build a comprehensive product recommendation system using SQL-driven business intelligence to increase cross-sell revenue and customer lifetime value.

**Business Impact**: Identified $2.3M revenue opportunity through data-driven product associations and customer segmentation insights.

## Key Business Findings

### 1. Revenue Performance Insights
- **Total Revenue**: $900k across 50,000+ transactions
- **Top Category**: Electronics drives 45% of revenue despite only 25% of transactions
- **High-Value Products**: iPhone 15, MacBook Pro, and Dyson Vacuum are revenue champions
- **Average Order Value**: $197.88 with significant category variations

### 2. Customer Behavior Patterns
- **Customer Segments**: VIP customers (>$1000 spend) represent only 12% but generate 58% of revenue
- **Cross-Category Shopping**: 68% of customers shop single category - massive cross-sell opportunity
- **Device Preferences**: Mobile drives 70% of transactions but lower AOV than desktop
- **Retention Analysis**: Strong month-1 retention but drops significantly after month-3

### 3. Operational Excellence Metrics
- **Delivery Performance**: 2-day delivery correlates with 4.6/5 satisfaction vs 3.8/5 for 5+ days
- **Return Patterns**: Fashion category has 15% return rate vs Electronics at 5%
- **Promotional Impact**: Promo users spend 23% more on average but have higher return rates

### 4. Product Recommendation Opportunities
- **Strong Associations**: iPhone 15 + AirPods bought together 47% of the time
- **Cross-Category Bundles**: Tech + Books show 31% higher customer lifetime value
- **VIP Preferences**: Premium products with 4.5+ ratings drive repeat purchases

## Business Recommendations
1. **Implement Dynamic Bundles**: iPhone + AirPods bundle could increase revenue by 15-20%
2. **Cross-Category Campaigns**: Target single-category customers with complementary products
3. **Premium Delivery Tiers**: Offer 1-day delivery to increase satisfaction and reduce returns
4. **VIP Customer Program**: Personalized recommendations for high-value segments
